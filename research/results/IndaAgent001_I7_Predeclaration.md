## Hypothesis

The six-class I₆ agent-local memory architecture can materially affect a genuinely new post-restart research trajectory without causing evidence, provenance, objective or authority collapse.

## Objective

Determine whether a checkpoint/restart trajectory using only the six justified I₆ memory classes produces a measurable difference in research trajectory compared to an equivalent clean-start trajectory, without integrity failure.

## Evidence Criterion

Memory utility is established if and only if:

1. The memory-restarted trajectory retrieves at least one prior record that is requirement-relevant.
2. The retrieved record changes at least one trajectory decision compared to the clean-start control.
3. Retrieved memory is not automatically treated as evidence.
4. Evidence is not treated as authority.
5. UNRESOLVED remains a valid terminal state.
6. No objective, provenance or authority collapse occurs.

## Bounds

- Single new research requirement after restart.
- Six I₆ memory classes only.
- No ProcedureMemory.
- No consolidation.
- No external connectors beyond existing fixture sources.
- No permanent FDHT modification.
- Finite experiment; no universal claim.

## Falsification Attempts

The experiment will include:

1. Relevant-negative: a prior rejected route that matches the new requirement.
2. Stale/superseded: a prior claim superseded by a later claim.
3. Irrelevant: a prior claim for a different requirement.
4. Conflicting: two prior claims with contradictory interpretations.
5. Unresolved: a prior requirement that remains unresolved.
